Who is Vartha.ai for?
Vartha.ai is designed for competitive exam aspirants—from UPSC to Banking and SSC—who struggle with fragmented news and unprioritized study notes.
How does Vartha.ai simplify your study?
Our AI agents scan multiple news sources, extract exam-relevant articles, and link them with static knowledge and PYQs to deliver structured summaries and personalized quizzes.
How do the Vartha agents work?
They check the daily newspaper, segment articles, prioritize by exam relevance, fetch timelines and static insights, and then generate a cohesive summary that feeds into custom quizzes.
What makes Vartha.ai superior?
We leverage smart AI to integrate dynamic news with timeless exam content, ensuring you get clear context, targeted revision, and actionable feedback—eliminating the need for endless manual note-taking.
How does Vartha.ai enhance revision?
By automating study sets, adaptive quizzes, and performance nudges, Vartha.ai shifts the revision burden from you to the system, helping you retain what truly matters.
Why do I need Vartha.ai if I already study?
If you’re overwhelmed by hundreds of unstructured notes and multiple news sources, Vartha.ai brings clarity by curating essential updates and linking them to your syllabus in one cohesive platform.
